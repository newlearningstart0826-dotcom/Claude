# Content calendar

Turn a niche, an audience and a goal into a concrete posting plan.

## Inputs

Ask only for what is missing:

1. **Niche / topic** — what the account is about.
2. **Audience** — who it is for and what they struggle with.
3. **Goal** — followers, leads, sales, or community.
4. **Cadence** — posts per week per platform (default: 4 on Instagram).
5. **Length** — how many weeks (default: 4).

## Steps

1. Pick 3–4 content pillars that tie the audience's problems to the goal.
2. Rotate formats across the week (carousel, reel, story, single image) so no
   format repeats on consecutive days.
3. For each slot write: date/day, platform, pillar, format, a working hook
   (under 12 words), and a one-line call to action.
4. Put one "proof" post (result, testimonial, behind-the-scenes) in every week.

## Output

A markdown table per week with columns
`Day | Platform | Pillar | Format | Hook | CTA`, followed by a short list of
the pillars and why each one serves the goal.
