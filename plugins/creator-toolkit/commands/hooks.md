Write 10 opening hooks for a short-form social post about: $ARGUMENTS

Rules:
- Each hook is under 12 words.
- Mix styles: question, bold claim, number/list, mistake, before/after, story opener.
- No hashtags, no emojis, no clickbait the post could not deliver on.

Return a numbered list, then mark the 3 strongest with ★ and say in one line why.
