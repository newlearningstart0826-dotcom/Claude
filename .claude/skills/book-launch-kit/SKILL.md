# Book Launch Kit

A complete launch and marketing skill that takes the finished (or near-finished) book and
produces every asset needed to bring it into the world — from back-cover copy to a 30-day
content plan.

The skill operates across **six asset categories**:

1. **Book Positioning Assets** — the core copy that defines the book everywhere it appears
2. **Retail & Discovery Copy** — Amazon/retailer descriptions, categories, keywords
3. **Author Platform Assets** — bio variants, one-pager, media kit, speaker pitch
4. **Launch Campaigns** — email sequences, social content calendar, ARC outreach
5. **Endorsement & PR Outreach** — blurb request emails, podcast pitches, press release
6. **Launch Strategy** — 30-day launch plan, pre-launch checklist, post-launch momentum

Run the full kit in one session, or invoke individual assets by name at any time.

## Before Building: Book Intake

If the author is coming from **book-architect**, much of this is already known — extract from
context, only ask for what's missing.

### Required Inputs

| Input | What to ask |
|---|---|
| **Book title & subtitle** | "What's the final (or working) title and subtitle?" |
| **Core premise** | "In one sentence: what does this book argue, show, or teach?" |
| **Target reader** | "Who is this book for — their role, situation, and what they want?" |
| **The transformation** | "What is the reader able to do, think, or feel after reading?" |
| **Author credentials** | "What makes you the right person to write this book?" |
| **Launch timing** | "When does the book publish, and what format? (self-pub / traditional / e-book)" |
| **Author platform** | "Where does your audience currently live? (LinkedIn / email / Instagram / etc.)" |

### Optional but powerful

| Input | What to ask |
|---|---|
| **Comparable titles** | "2–3 books yours sits alongside in the market" |
| **Origin story** | "The personal moment or professional insight that started this book" |
| **A favourite idea** | "The one part of the book you most want people to talk about" |
| **Known endorsers** | "Anyone in your network — or dream list — who might endorse this" |
| **Speaking/media goals** | "Is a speaking career or media presence part of why you wrote this?" |

Once intake is complete, present a **Launch Brief**:

```
LAUNCH BRIEF
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TITLE:          [Full title + subtitle]
PREMISE:        [One sentence]
READER:         [Profile]
TRANSFORMATION: [What the reader becomes]
AUTHOR HOOK:    [Credential + origin story in one sentence]
FORMAT/TIMING:  [Publication details]
PLATFORM:       [Where the author's audience lives]
COMP TITLES:    [2–3 comparable books]
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

Ask: *"Does this capture it? Any adjustments before I build the kit?"*

## Category 1 — Book Positioning Assets

The foundation everything else is built on. Get these right first — they inform all other copy.

### 1A — The Logline

A single sentence capturing the book's promise. Used everywhere: social bios, verbal pitches,
email signatures, media appearances.

**Formula:** *[Who] + [what struggle/situation] + [what the book gives them] + [so they can X]*

Produce **3 logline options** at different lengths:
- **Tweet-length** (under 20 words): for social bios and verbal pitches
- **Intro-length** (30–40 words): for email signatures and author bios
- **Pitch-length** (50–60 words): for media kits and speaking pitches

---

### 1B — The Back Cover Copy

The most-read piece of copy after the title. Must do five jobs in ~200 words: hook the reader,
establish the problem, promise the solution, prove authority, and drive the purchase decision.

**Structure:**
```
[HOOK — 1–2 sentences]
A provocative question, a bold claim, or a scene that drops the
reader into the book's world.

[THE PROBLEM — 2–3 sentences]
Name the pain, gap, or broken belief the book addresses.
Make the reader feel seen before offering the solution.

[THE PROMISE — 2–3 sentences]
What the book delivers. Be specific. "You'll learn how to..."
beats "This book will help you..." every time.

[THE PROOF — 1–2 sentences]
Why this author. One credibility sentence — not a full bio.

[THE CLOSE — 1 sentence]
The line that makes them turn the book over and buy it.
```

Produce the full back cover copy. Then produce **1 alternative version** with a different
hook style (if Version A opens with a question, Version B opens with a scene).

---

### 1C — The Elevator Pitch

30-second verbal pitch for events, podcasts, meetings. Sounds spoken, not written.
No jargon, no hedging. Format: problem → what the book does → who it's for → the result.

Also produce a **60-second extended version** for longer introductions.

---

### 1D — The Origin Story (100 words)

The personal or professional moment that made the author write this book. Used in: author
bios, keynote introductions, podcast openers. Not a credential list — a *moment*. The more
specific and human, the more it builds trust.

## Category 2 — Retail & Discovery Copy

### 2A — Amazon/Retailer Long Description (400–600 words)

The most SEO-critical copy the author controls. Uses HTML formatting (bold, line breaks).

**Structure:**
```
OPENING HOOK (bold) — 1–2 lines

THE PROBLEM SECTION
2 paragraphs on the pain/gap the book addresses.
Specific enough that the target reader thinks "this is about me."

WHAT YOU'LL DISCOVER
Bullet list of 5–8 specific takeaways — concrete deliverables,
not vague promises. Each starts with a strong active verb:
• Discover how to...
• Learn the three-step process for...
• Understand why most [audience] fail at [X] — and the one fix

ABOUT THE AUTHOR (2–3 sentences)
Credentials + proof + one humanising detail.

CLOSING CTA
"If you're ready to [transformation], [Title] is your guide."
```

### 2B — Short Description (150 words)

For aggregators, library systems, anywhere the long description is truncated. Must
standalone and convert without the bullets.

### 2C — Keyword & Category Strategy

Produce:
- **7 Amazon backend keywords** (phrases readers search when looking for this type of book)
- **2 primary categories** (for best placement)
- **3 secondary categories** (where the book could rank for bestseller status)

## Category 3 — Author Platform Assets

### 3A — Author Bio Variants

Produce **4 bio lengths** in the same voice:

| Version | Length | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **One-liner** | 1 sentence | Social media profile headers |
| **Short** | 50 words | Podcast guest intros, event programs |
| **Standard** | 100–120 words | Book back cover, media kit, speaker bio |
| **Full** | 250 words | Website About page, press kit |

Each bio structure (scaled to length): what the author *does* → their *proof* →
their *human element* → the book, naturally woven in.

### 3B — One-Page Book Summary

A single-page document for corporate buyers, speaking bureaus, and media bookers.

```
[TITLE + SUBTITLE]
[TAGLINE — the logline]

THE BOOK IN ONE PARAGRAPH (75 words)

THE PROBLEM IT SOLVES
[3 bullet points — reader's situation before this book]

WHAT READERS GAIN
[3–5 specific, concrete outcome bullets]

WHO THIS BOOK IS FOR
[2–3 specific reader profiles]

ABOUT THE AUTHOR
[Standard bio]

PRAISE / EARLY ENDORSEMENTS
[1–2 blurbs, or placeholder]

CONTACT / ORDER INFORMATION
```

### 3C — Media Kit One-Pager

For journalists, podcast hosts, event organisers.

**Sections:**
- Book overview (title, pub date, publisher, format, price)
- "Why this book, why now" — the cultural or professional moment the book speaks to
- 5 talking points (angles a journalist or podcaster could build a segment around)
- 3–5 suggested interview questions
- Author bio (standard version)
- High-resolution assets note + contact information

### 3D — Speaker Pitch One-Pager

For speaking bureaus, event organisers, and conference bookers.

**Sections:**
- Speaker positioning line (what makes this author's talk unique in the market)
- 3 talk titles with one-paragraph descriptions (each a different angle from the book)
- Ideal audience / event type
- What audiences leave with (3 bullet outcomes)
- Author credibility (speaker-focused — previous stages, audiences reached, media)
- Booking contact

## Category 4 — Launch Campaigns

### 4A — Pre-Launch Email Sequence (5 emails)

For authors with an email list. Builds anticipation over 4–6 weeks before publication.

```
EMAIL 1 — "I've been working on something" (6 weeks out)
  Purpose: Seed awareness. No hard sell. Personal story of why this book exists.
  CTA: Invite them to reply with their biggest challenge on this topic.

EMAIL 2 — "Here's what's inside" (4 weeks out)
  Purpose: Build desire. Share 1 key insight or chapter idea from the book.
  CTA: Pre-order link (if available) or "join the launch list."

EMAIL 3 — "Can I ask you a favour?" (2 weeks out)
  Purpose: ARC / review cultivation. Offer advance copy for honest review.
  CTA: Reply to claim an ARC.

EMAIL 4 — "It's here" (launch day)
  Purpose: Purchase driver. Direct, warm, specific. Why today matters.
  CTA: Buy link + review request.

EMAIL 5 — "What readers are saying" (1 week post-launch)
  Purpose: Social proof + second wave. Share early reactions and reviews.
  CTA: Buy link for those who haven't + ask buyers to leave a review.
```

For each email, produce: subject line (+ 1 alternative A/B test option), full email body,
and CTA copy.

### 4B — Social Content Calendar (30 Days)

A complete month of launch content across the author's primary platform. Calibrated to
where their audience lives (LinkedIn / Instagram / newsletter / Twitter-X).

**Content mix across 30 days:**
- **Idea posts** (40%) — a standalone insight, argument, or counterintuitive claim from
  the book. Delivers real value — not "buy my book."
- **Behind-the-scenes** (20%) — the writing process, what surprised them, what got cut
- **Reader-focused** (20%) — questions, polls, "does this resonate?" — builds conversation
  and surfaces buyer intent
- **Direct promotion** (20%) — buy links, review requests, launch week urgency —
  earned by the value posts surrounding them

**Output format (all 30 posts written out):**

```
WEEK 1 — PRE-LAUNCH WARMUP

Day 1 — [Platform]
  Type:    Idea post
  Hook:    [First line — the most important sentence]
  Body:    [Full post copy]
  CTA:     [What to do]

Day 2 — [Platform]
  [...]
```

### 4C — ARC (Advance Review Copy) Campaign

A system for seeding reviews before and at launch.

**Deliverables:**
- **ARC outreach email** (to send to existing network / list)
- **ARC landing page copy** (for BookFunnel or similar)
- **Reviewer briefing note** (what to do with the ARC, how to leave a review, timeline)
- **Follow-up email** (2 weeks post-distribution — gentle review reminder)
- **Thank-you email** (after review is posted)

## Category 5 — Endorsement & PR Outreach

### 5A — Endorsement Request Email

The blurb-request email for potential endorsers. Must be:
- Short (under 200 words — they are busy)
- Personal (reference something specific about them)
- Low-friction (clear ask, clear deadline, offer key chapter excerpts)
- Confident (peer-to-peer, not supplicating)

Produce a **template with [PERSONALISATION PLACEHOLDER]** sections. Then produce
**2 variations**: one for a close contact, one for cold outreach.

Also produce: a 1-paragraph excerpt package description — the 3–5 key passages to send
an endorser who doesn't have time to read the whole book.

### 5B — Podcast Pitch Email

A cold pitch to podcast hosts. Must:
- Reference a specific episode of their show (no generic pitches)
- Hook on the author's expertise angle, not the book title
- Offer 3 specific talking points that make a great episode
- Be under 150 words

Produce a **pitch template** with clear [PERSONALISE HERE] flags. Then produce a
**fully written example** for a hypothetical podcast in the book's genre.

Produce **5 episode angle titles** — framed as episode titles, not chapter names.
*(These show the host what the episode looks like before they agree to it.)*

### 5C — Press Release

Standard format for local/trade media. Inverted pyramid — most important information first.

```
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

[HEADLINE — the news hook, not the book title]
[SUBHEADLINE — the who/what/why]

[CITY, Date] — [Opening: who, what, when, where, why in 2–3 sentences]

[BODY ¶1 — the book's argument and why it matters now]
[BODY ¶2 — author quote — punchy, not generic]
[BODY ¶3 — author credentials + brief book details]
[BODY ¶4 — availability, price, format, where to buy]

ABOUT [AUTHOR NAME]
[Standard bio]

###

MEDIA CONTACT:   [Name / email / phone]
REVIEW COPIES:   [How to request]
HIGH-RES ASSETS: [Available at URL or on request]
```

## Category 6 — Launch Strategy

### 6A — 30-Day Launch Plan

Week-by-week action plan from 4 weeks before launch to 2 weeks after.

```
LAUNCH MASTER PLAN — [Book Title]
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
LAUNCH DATE: [Date]
GOAL:        [e.g., 500 copies sold in launch week / hit #1 in X category]

WEEK -4 (4 weeks before)
  □ Send ARC emails to inner circle
  □ Finalise Amazon listing — all copy live
  □ Schedule social content weeks 1–2
  □ Send Email 1 to list
  □ Confirm podcast bookings for launch week

WEEK -2 (2 weeks before)
  □ Send ARC follow-up to anyone who hasn't posted
  □ Send Email 2 to list
  □ Schedule social content weeks 3–4
  □ Brief launch partners / referral contacts

WEEK -1 (launch prep)
  □ Brief anyone doing a launch-week share
  □ Send Email 3 (ARC / review request)
  □ Final Amazon listing check
  □ Prepare launch day message

LAUNCH WEEK
  □ Day 1: Send Email 4 + post across all platforms
  □ Days 2–3: Engage with every comment, share, and review posted
  □ Days 4–5: Second-wave social content
  □ Day 7: Send Email 5 (social proof round-up)

WEEK +2 (post-launch momentum)
  □ Identify top 5 reviews — share with attribution
  □ Pitch 2–3 more podcasts with launch-week social proof
  □ Plan month-2 content based on what resonated most
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

### 6B — Pre-Launch Checklist

Everything that must be in place before launch day.

```
PRE-LAUNCH CHECKLIST — [Book Title]
══════════════════════════════════════
RETAIL SETUP
  □ Amazon listing live — long description, keywords, categories
  □ Author Central profile complete with bio and photo
  □ "Look Inside" pages are strong (cover, TOC, first chapter)
  □ Price confirmed across all formats

AUTHOR PLATFORM
  □ Website updated — book page, buy links, press kit
  □ All social bios updated to reference the book
  □ One-page PDF ready to send on request
  □ Media kit ready for podcasts and journalists

EMAIL LIST
  □ Email 1 sent (6 weeks out)
  □ ARC distributed (4 weeks out)
  □ Full email sequence scheduled

SOCIAL CONTENT
  □ 30-day calendar approved and scheduled (weeks 1–2 minimum live)
  □ Launch week posts ready

PR & OUTREACH
  □ Endorsement emails sent (8 weeks out)
  □ At least 2–3 blurbs confirmed
  □ Podcast pitches sent to 10+ shows
  □ At least 2 podcast recordings completed before launch

ASSETS
  □ High-resolution cover image (3000px minimum)
  □ Professional author headshot
  □ 3–5 quote cards (key book lines formatted for social)
══════════════════════════════════════
```

### 6C — Post-Launch Momentum Plan

Extends visibility from launch month into months 2–6.

- **Weeks 5–8**: Second wave podcast interviews (use launch reviews as proof)
- **Month 2**: Repurpose 1 chapter into a long-form article for a major publication
- **Month 3**: Run a reader challenge or live event around the book's core framework
- **Month 4–6**: Corporate / bulk purchase outreach — 10 organisations whose teams benefit
- **Ongoing**: Build a "reader results" bank — stories from readers who applied the ideas

## Integration with the Book Writing Pipeline

This skill is the final stage of the full pipeline:

```
voice-calibrator → book-architect → research-evidence-builder
                          ↓
                   chapter-builder
                          ↓
       beta-reader-simulator ←→ editor-sharpener
                          ↓
                  book-launch-kit
                      (Launch)
```

**From book-architect**, carry forward: Working Title, Core Premise, Reader Promise, Target
Reader, Unique Angle, Tone & Style, Comparable Titles — these feed directly into all
positioning and retail copy.

**From voice-calibrator**, carry forward: the Voice Fingerprint — all launch copy (emails,
social posts, bios) should sound like the author, not like generic marketing language.

After producing the full kit, offer:
> "Want me to prioritise which assets to build first based on your timeline and platform?"
